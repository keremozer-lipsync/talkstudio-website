Features

You don't have to make animations manually.

AniStudioLS is designed to automate the most labor-intensive part of the process of creating 2D characters that speak.

◉

Auto Lip-Sync

It analyses the audio recording and automatically places the appropriate mouth shapes on the timeline.

◇

Special Characters

Create personalised animations using your own character images and mouth shapes.

◎

Human Voice Detection

By distinguishing between spoken segments, it reduces unnecessary mouth movements during periods of silence and non-speech soundsr.

◆

Expression System

It provides the infrastructure to add facial expressions and different animation states to the dialogue timeline.

▣

Long-Term Record Support

It makes processing and video production more manageable by splitting long audio files into segments.

↗

Video Export

Export the animation you have created as a video at your chosen resolution (There may be resolution issues in the beta version).
